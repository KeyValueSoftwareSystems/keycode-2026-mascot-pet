Keycode Pet

A small pixel character that lives on your desktop. It runs along the bottom of your screen over whatever you are working on, reminds you to drink water, and the whole team can make every installed pet say something by editing one JSON file.

Download for macOS, Windows or Linux →

This app is not code-signed. macOS and Windows will both warn you the first time you open it. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open rather than double-clicking. You only do it once. Full instructions .

Privacy

The app makes one network request: it fetches manifest.json from this site so the team can broadcast a message to everyone. No analytics, no identifiers, no usage data, no accounts. Nothing is ever uploaded from your machine unless you use “Report a problem…” and send it yourself.

For the team

manifest.json on this page is what every installed pet polls. Publishing a message is pnpm notify "…", which commits it — so anything that appears on a colleague's screen goes through the same review as code.
